AniPasta respects the rights of copyright owners and reviews properly submitted copyright notices concerning links or references shown on the website.

Notice

AniPasta does not claim ownership of third-party anime or other media. Some pages may index, organize or display embedded links served by external providers.

Required Information

  1. Your name and valid contact email.
  2. Evidence that you own the relevant rights or are authorized to act for the owner.
  3. A clear description of the work concerned.
  4. The exact AniPasta URL or search result involved.
  5. A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or applicable law.
  6. A statement that the information supplied is accurate and that you are authorized to act.
  7. Your electronic signature.

Submit a Notice

Send complete requests to pastah2o@proton.me.

Review

We aim to review valid requests promptly, normally within 4-5 business days. Where appropriate, AniPasta may remove or disable a listing or link from its index.

External hosts control files on their own servers, so the underlying material may also need to be reported directly to the service that hosts it.

False Notices

Please submit copyright notices only when you have a genuine rights-based complaint. Knowingly false or misleading information may delay review.

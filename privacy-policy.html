Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information AniPasta may process when you use https://anipasta.top.

Consent

By using AniPasta, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. Where consent is legally required for a particular activity, we will seek it through the relevant service or interface.

Information We Collect

AniPasta's account system is powered by Supabase. When you create and use an account, the service may process information needed for authentication and account operation, including your email address and username.

When you use account features, AniPasta may store favourites and watch history associated with your account so those features can work across sessions.

If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name and the contents of your message or attachments.

Passwords and Authentication

Your account password is submitted to the authentication service for account sign-in. AniPasta does not need to display or directly read your password in order to provide account features.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate and maintain the website.
  • Authenticate accounts and keep users signed in.
  • Store and display favourites and watch history requested by the user.
  • Respond to support and copyright requests.
  • Improve reliability, security and functionality.

Log Files

AniPasta does not currently use Google Analytics or a similar analytics platform. However, hosting, security, authentication or infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps or request details as part of operating their services.

Cookies and Browser Storage

AniPasta and its authentication services may use cookies, local storage or similar browser technologies for login sessions, essential functionality and user preferences. You can control browser storage and cookies through your browser settings, although disabling essential storage may affect account functionality.

Advertising

AniPasta currently does not display advertising. The site may introduce limited advertising in the future to help cover hosting, bandwidth and maintenance costs. If advertising is introduced, the applicable third-party provider's privacy practices will also apply to data processed through its advertising technologies.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, video hosts, embeds, advertising services or other external providers. Review their respective privacy policies before using those services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account information, favourites and watch history are stored while your account is active. To help limit database storage, accounts with no meaningful activity for 30 consecutive days may be treated as inactive and automatically deleted, together with their associated favourites, watch history and profile data. Meaningful activity can include signing in, watching anime, updating watch history, using favourites, or making relevant account/profile changes.

You may contact us to ask about account-related data or deletion requests. Some information may also remain in provider backups or security logs for a limited period according to the provider's policies.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us and we will review requests where applicable law gives you such rights.

Children's Information

AniPasta does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through its account registration flow. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Contacting Us

For privacy questions or requests, email pastah2o@proton.me or visit our Contact Us page.

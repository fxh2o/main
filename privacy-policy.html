Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information AniPasta may process when you use https://anipasta.top.

Consent

By using AniPasta, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. Where consent is legally required for a particular activity, we will seek it through the relevant service or interface.

Information We Collect

AniPasta does not require user accounts or account registration to browse, search or watch anime.

Watch history is stored locally in your browser on the device you use. AniPasta also uses local browser storage for notification read state and other essential site preferences.

If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name and the contents of your message or attachments.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate and maintain the website.
  • Store local watch history and essential browser preferences.
  • Display site notifications and announcements.
  • Respond to support and copyright requests.
  • Improve reliability, security and functionality.

Log Files

AniPasta does not currently use Google Analytics or a similar analytics platform. However, hosting, security, media, database or infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps or request details as part of operating their services.

Cookies and Browser Storage

AniPasta uses local storage for essential browser functionality such as watch history and notification state. Third-party services used for hosting, media playback or linked content may use their own cookies or browser technologies under their own policies.

Advertising

AniPasta currently does not display advertising. The site may introduce limited advertising in the future to help cover hosting, bandwidth and maintenance costs. If advertising is introduced, the applicable third-party provider's privacy practices will also apply to data processed through its advertising technologies.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, video hosts, embeds, advertising services or other external providers. Review their respective privacy policies before using those services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local watch history remains in your browser until you clear it through the site or your browser settings. Information submitted through direct contact remains subject to the nature of the request and the policies of the services used to receive it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us and we will review requests where applicable law gives you such rights.

Children's Information

AniPasta does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Contacting Us

For privacy questions or requests, email pastah2o@proton.me or visit our Contact Us page.
